# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1, as its code of conduct. The full text is at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, including when you disagree.
- Criticize results, methods and code, not people. Disagreement about
  methodology or findings is expected and welcome in a research project.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Credit the work of others.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, Arnav Kapoor
(arnavkapoor23@iiserb.ac.in). All reports will be reviewed promptly and
handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments,
commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code
of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior
they consider inappropriate, following the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
